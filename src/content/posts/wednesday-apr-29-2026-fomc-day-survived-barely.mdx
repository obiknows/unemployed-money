## POST GAME 💰⛹🏿‍♂️

##### SO WHAT DID WE LEARN

* **FOMC DAYS ARE NOT HIGH PROBABILITY DAYS.**
  market waits. tape chops.
  0DTEs bleed. size down or sit out
  until after the announcement.
  this isn't a new lesson.
  apply it next FOMC.
* **ADMIN SIGNALS NEED AN EXIT RULE.**
  i can take text signals.
  but i need to define before entry:
  *if this is wrong by X, i'm out.*
  no exit rule = holding an AAPL 0DTE
  for 5 hours on a sideways FOMC day.
* **WAIT FOR MANIPULATION ON 0DTES.**
  TSLA was a sideways day.
  entered before the 30m ORB fake break.
  same lesson as last week.
  it keeps showing up until we fix it.
* **THE CONDOR WORKED. LEARN THE STRUCTURE.**
  even reactive, even late,
  the iron condor was the right read
  on a post-FOMC range.
  get comfortable building these
  earlier in the session on range days.
* **SURVIVED FOMC. THAT'S SOMETHING.**
  -$111 on one of the hardest
  trading days of the month.
  we've had worse days on easy tapes.
