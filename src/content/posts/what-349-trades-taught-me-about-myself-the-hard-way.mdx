*this isn't a journal entry. this is a reckoning.*

after the worst trading week of my year — -$2,595 in 5 days, 25.6% win rate, second worst week since February — i did something i should've done months ago.

i sat down. exported everything. fed it all into Claude and Tradezella. and asked for the truth.

no sugarcoating. no vibes. just data.

what came back was uncomfortable. illuminating. and honestly? exactly what i needed.

this is that report.

![](/images/posts/what-349-trades-taught-me-about-myself-the-hard-way/Screenshot%202026-04-27%20at%2011.37.50%E2%80%AFPM.png)

**YTD Net P\&L: +$494** **Win Rate: 38.4%** **Max Drawdown: -$28,160** **All-Time Peak: +$13,506**

on the surface, +$494 sounds fine. barely positive but hey, green is green.

until you look at the equity curve.

peaked at $13,506 in late January. crashed to -$28,160 at the worst point. and crawled back to almost exactly zero.

that's not a trading account. that's a roller coaster that starts and ends in the same place and scares the shit out of you the whole ride.

something is deeply wrong with the process. the data told me exactly what.

## the single most important finding

![](/images/posts/what-349-trades-taught-me-about-myself-the-hard-way/Screenshot%202026-04-27%20at%2011.38.16%E2%80%AFPM.png)

this one stopped me cold.

**Entry Time Window — YTD Net P\&L:**

| Window      | Net      | WR    |
| ----------- | -------- | ----- |
| 9:30-9:44   | -$19,272 | 41.7% |
| 9:45-9:59   | +$3,654  | 45%   |
| 10:00-10:29 | +$1,069  | 43.8% |
| 11:00-11:59 | +$8,935  | 28.6% |
| 12:00-12:59 | +$4,626  | 22.2% |

the first 15 minutes of the session have cost me **$19,272 this year.**

every single other time window is profitable.

let me say that again slower.

**9:30 to 9:44 is the only time window losing money. every window after that is green.**

and it gets worse when you zoom in:

![](/images/posts/what-349-trades-taught-me-about-myself-the-hard-way/Screenshot%202026-04-27%20at%2011.38.30%E2%80%AFPM.png)

**9:30-9:34 alone: -$18,198 YTD. 24 trades.**

one five-minute window. responsible for more losses than my entire year's gain — **36 times over.**

if i had simply waited 10 minutes before entering a single trade this year?

**+$20,313 YTD instead of +$494.**

the 30-minute ORB rule i wrote about in this very blog. the rule i identified. documented. and keep violating every single morning.

the data ran the simulation. the answer isn't even 30 minutes.

**don't trade the first 10 minutes. after 9:40 you're fine.**

waiting 15 min → +$19,766. waiting 10 min → +$20,313. ← optimal. waiting 30 min → +$16,112. (you actually leave edge on the table by waiting too long because 9:45-9:59 is one of your best windows.)

the rule writes itself.

## the 18 rules

these came from two places: the data and my own words. most of them i already wrote somewhere in this blog. the data just confirmed they were right.

**1. wait 10 minutes. no trades before 9:40.** the first 10 minutes have cost $18,198. every other window is green.

**2. hard daily stop: -$500. laptop closes.** simulation shows -$500 stop turns +$494 YTD into +$30,028. i identified this rule on april 21st. it still doesn't mechanically exist. that changes now.

**3. first trade red → seriously consider stopping.** win-start days average +$708. loss-start days average -$440. the first trade sets the whole session.

**4. monday = fact finding. max 3 trades, 1 contract.** -$15,789 YTD, 28.8% WR. size like you know this.

**5. no voice chat while trading.** text signals only. evaluate every call against your own system before entering. if it doesn't fit, skip it.

**6. use your system. every single time.** 30m ORB → mark the range. ICT Power of 3 → wait for manipulation. first FVG → retracement entry. after. not before. not during. after.

**7. trim into profit. always.** when contracts are working early, take some off. greed + theta + headlines = thursday april 23rd.

**8. honor the stop on admin signals.** if the admin who called the trade exits — you exit. holding past someone else's stop is not strategy.

**9. never flip bias without a new thesis.** losing the put doesn't make the call right. no new information = no new trade.

**10. never go further OTM to save premium.** cheaper entry is not better entry when you're fighting the tape.

**11. move to 3-7 DTE as primary vehicle.** 7+ DTE: 53.6% WR, +$10,322. 1-2 DTE: 29.7% WR, -$3,453. the data is not subtle.

**12. 1-2 contracts max until the daily stop rule is mechanical.** 1-2 contracts: +$8K YTD. 6+ contracts: -$9.7K YTD. the size is killing the edge.

**13. META is the edge. protect it.** 95 trades, 40% WR, +$5,032. most traded and best performing. understand why and replicate it.

**14. stop trading NVDA until you can explain the specific edge.** 18 trades, 16.7% WR, -$983. that's not a name — that's a habit.

**15. swing is the alpha. don't erase it.** swing: +$2,911, 51.8% WR. day trading: -$2,417, 35.2% WR. the long book works. the day session is eating it.

**16. add a time stop to every thesis.** if the level doesn't do what it's supposed to by noon — the trade is wrong. get out. don't wait for confirmation that never comes.

**17. first trade loss → log it, reassess, consider stopping.** don't recover. don't revenge. reassess.

**18. january is the blueprint.** PF 3.36, +$9.2K, avg winner $668 vs avg loser $111. that ratio is achievable. i did it once. i can do it again. the market didn't change. the discipline did.

## what's actually changing

not vibes. not intentions. mechanical rules.

**before the session:**

* mark the 30m ORB high and low
* identify the first FVG on the 1H
* write down the bias and what would invalidate it
* set a -$500 daily stop in the platform if possible

**during the session:**

* no trades before 9:40
* 1-2 contracts only
* no voice chat — text signals evaluated against my system only
* first trade red → log the reason and seriously consider stopping

**after the session:**

* journal entry same day
* tag every trade with what rule it followed or violated
* weekly review with Tradezella + Claude every Sunday
